## Detailed description — copy from here

Touchgrass helps you understand your AI usage and make more deliberate choices
about the models you use.

Enable the supported AI websites you want to track. Touchgrass records usage
information and shows contextual coaching directly on those pages, even when its
panel is closed. When a task may suit a lighter model, it can offer a switch or
help you move the prompt to another enabled AI website.

WHAT YOU CAN DO

• View AI usage summaries, activity trends, scores and streaks.
• Receive contextual suggestions about when a lighter model may fit your task.
• Choose whether to follow a suggestion or continue with your current model.
• Review estimates of environmental impact associated with recorded AI activity.
• Upload a supported conversation export for additional analysis.
• Personalize your experience with Pip, Touchgrass's companion.

HOW IT WORKS

Choose supported websites during onboarding and grant their site permissions.
Open an enabled website and use it normally. The coach appears when its decision
rules identify an appropriate opportunity; it does not make a suggestion for
every prompt. Model switching depends on what the website and your account make
available. Access to supported AI websites may require separate accounts or
subscriptions.

DATA AND ONLINE FEATURES

Touchgrass stores preferences and usage history, including prompt snippets, in
your browser. Online coaching can send draft text and limited conversation
context to our backend and AI service providers. Export analysis processes files
you choose to upload. Automatic integration repair can send page snapshots and
draft excerpts when an enabled website changes. See our privacy policy for the
information involved, providers, retention and available controls.

RECOMMENDATIONS AND ESTIMATES

Model recommendations are guidance, not guarantees of accuracy, quality or
savings. A lighter-model recommendation does not mean every model in that class
has been individually benchmarked for your task. Environmental figures are
estimates rather than measurements of your individual request's actual energy
or emissions. Website interfaces and available models can change.

Touchgrass is an independent project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by
the AI websites it integrates with.
