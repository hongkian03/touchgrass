## The coach does not appear

Check that Touchgrass is enabled, onboarding is complete and the website is
selected. In Chrome's extension settings, confirm that Touchgrass has access to
the site. Refresh a page that was open before installation or an update.

Recommendations depend on the prompt, context, available models and cooldowns.
Not every prompt will produce a recommendation. The extension panel does not
need to remain open for the coach to work on an enabled website.

## A model switch does not complete

The target model must be available in your AI-site account. Subscriptions, model
availability and website interface changes can affect switching. Touchgrass may
leave the site's model menu open for you to finish the selection.

## Analysis fails or remains queued

Check your connection and whether the service reports an error. Export analysis
depends on our hosted backend and external execution/model services. Contact us
with the job ID, approximate time, browser version, extension version and error
category. Do not send a full export unless we establish a suitable support path
and you understand what information it contains.

## Are recommendations and impact figures exact?

No. Model recommendations are guidance, and not every destination model has been
individually evaluated for your task. Environmental figures are estimates rather
than direct measurements of a particular request's actual energy or emissions.
Review important outputs yourself.

## Is everything processed locally?

No. Preferences and local usage history live in Chrome extension storage. Online
coaching sends drafts and limited context for classification/routing. Analysis
uploads selected exports. Automatic repair can send page snapshots and draft
excerpts. See the privacy policy for the relevant services and retention.

## How do I stop activity on a website?

Remove it from your enabled platforms and revoke Touchgrass's access to that
website in Chrome's extension settings. You can also disable or uninstall the
extension. These actions stop future extension activity but do not automatically
erase previously processed server data.

## How do I delete information?

The current Settings → Reset control resets core usage and score information but
preserves your name and enabled platforms. It is not an erase-all control.
Uninstalling removes active extension storage from that Chrome profile, but not
backend records, saved reports, browser history or independent backups.

For server-side access or deletion requests, contact [PRIVACY_EMAIL]. Include
only the minimum nonsecret information needed to locate the relevant records,
such as an approximate date or request/job ID. Never send passwords, provider API
keys or extension session tokens. We may need to verify a request before acting.

## What should I include in a bug report?

- Chrome version and operating system.
- Touchgrass version and the affected AI website.
- Steps to reproduce using a nonsensitive example.
- Approximate timestamp and request/job ID if available.
- A screenshot with names, messages, keys and identifiers removed where unnecessary.

Do not attach unredacted conversation exports or full page snapshots to public
issues or store reviews.
